What we hunt for

Some things point straight at you. Others only give you away in a crowd: a city plus a job plus an age is usually enough.

Points straight at you
  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Birth dates
  • Bank accounts
  • Social handles
Points at you in a crowd
  • Places
  • Companies and organisations
  • Ages
  • Dates
  • Links

TMI runs the AnonymizeIt pipeline: pattern rules, spaCy and Presidio. English only. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking.

It's a mirror, not a lawyer. It misses most street addresses and some ID numbers, and now and then it thinks a band is a company.
