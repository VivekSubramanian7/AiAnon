The quick frisk

Pattern rules for emails, phones, bank numbers, birthdays and links, plus word lists for names, places and companies. About 1 MB. Done in seconds.

02

The detective

An AI name-spotter (bert-base-NER) that reads names it has never seen. 104 MB, downloaded once if you ask for it, then it works offline too.

TMI runs the AnonymizeIt rules inside your browser. The detective is bert-base-NER (MIT), loaded from Hugging Face with Transformers.js. Names: US Census Bureau 1990. Places: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). English only. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking.

It's a mirror, not a lawyer. It misses most street addresses and health details, and now and then it thinks a band is a company.
